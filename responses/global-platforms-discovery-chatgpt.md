- **Technical Architecture (4/5):** DTCC describes its platform as an open-source environment for creating, simulating, and visualizing digital twins of cities, and its public repositories show modular packages for core, IO, building, simulation, and viewer functions ([official site](https://www.dtcc.chalmers.se/), [GitHub org](https://github.com/dtcc-platform)).

- **Openness & Licensing (5/5):** The platform is openly developed and multiple core repositories are released under [MIT](https://github.com/dtcc-platform/dtcc) ([builder](https://github.com/dtcc-platform/dtcc-builder), [core](https://github.com/dtcc-platform/dtcc-core), [sim](https://github.com/dtcc-platform/dtcc-sim)).

- **City-Scale Capability (4/5):** DTCC’s own framing is city-first and covers modeling, simulation, visualization, and interactive urban planning, which squarely fits UDT scope ([official site](https://www.dtcc.chalmers.se/), [GitHub org](https://github.com/dtcc-platform)).

- **Maturity & Adoption (3/5):** DTCC is beyond a toy prototype and has documentation plus multiple packages, but it still reads as a research-to-practice platform rather than a globally established commercial deployment stack ([documentation site](https://platform.dtcc.chalmers.se/), [Vinnova project page](https://www.vinnova.se/en/p/digital-twin-cities/)).

- **Integration Posture (4/5):** The modular package breakdown and open viewer indicate good composability, though standards and external API documentation are lighter than in older geospatial platforms ([viewer repo](https://github.com/dtcc-platform/dtcc-web-viewer), [io repo](https://github.com/dtcc-platform/dtcc-io)).

- **Governance (4/5):** Governance is public and institution-backed: DTCC is run by a university-hosted center with public code and public funding references from [Vinnova](https://www.vinnova.se/en/p/digital-twin-cities/).

## vCity

- **Organization:** Barcelona Supercomputing Center is identified by the project as leading the platform’s development ([official post](https://www.vcity.tech/2024/12/17/eurocities-academy-and-vcity-promote-digital-innovation-training-on-urban-digital-twins/))

- **Link:** [vCity](https://www.vcity.tech/)

- **License:** unknown ([official site](https://www.vcity.tech/))

- **Type:** Human-centric urban digital twin platform ([official about page](https://www.vcity.tech/about/))

- **Inclusion criterion:** Explicit UDT

- **Technical Architecture (4/5):** vCity describes itself as a data-driven urban digital twin platform for assessing the effects of interventions on city life, with simulation, what-if tools, and data-driven policy support ([homepage](https://www.vcity.tech/), [about page](https://www.vcity.tech/about/)).

- **Openness & Licensing (2/5):** The website shares papers and technical resources, but I could not verify an open-source software repository or license for the core platform itself from the official sources reviewed ([resources page](https://www.vcity.tech/resources/)).

- **City-Scale Capability (4/5):** The project explicitly targets urban policy questions across air quality, services, energy efficiency, mobility, and broader city-life impacts, which is well inside UDT scope ([homepage](https://www.vcity.tech/), [project update](https://www.vcity.tech/2025/04/15/our-researchers-patricio-reyes-and-alex-gil-represented-us-at-the-presentation-of-the-program-generation-d-building-generation-ai/)).

- **Maturity & Adoption (3/5):** vCity is active, externally presented, and used in city-facing programs, but the public evidence still looks more like a research-backed emerging platform than a mature product line ([Eurocities training post](https://www.vcity.tech/2024/12/17/eurocities-academy-and-vcity-promote-digital-innovation-training-on-urban-digital-twins/), [OECD mention post](https://www.vcity.tech/2025/01/29/vcity-highlights-in-oecd-report-global-trends-in-government-innovation-2024/)).

- **Integration Posture (3/5):** The project clearly works with urban datasets and external city partners, but public API, standards, or self-hosting documentation are not yet very visible from the official site ([resources page](https://www.vcity.tech/resources/)).

- **Governance (3/5):** Governance appears to be institution-led around [Barcelona Supercomputing Center](https://www.vcity.tech/2024/12/17/eurocities-academy-and-vcity-promote-digital-innovation-training-on-urban-digital-twins/) and public funding programs, which is stronger than a closed startup but less open than a public code consortium.
