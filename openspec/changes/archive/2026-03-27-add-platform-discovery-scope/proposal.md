## Why

`docs/methodology.md` defines inclusion criteria at a high level but does not provide an operationalised discovery scope — a working document that sets a platform target, names known anchor platforms, and gives explicit exclusion examples to calibrate the boundary before research begins. Without this, different research sessions may apply the criteria inconsistently.

## What Changes

- Add `docs/scope.md` as a discovery scope document containing:
  - A restatement of the inclusion criteria in operational terms (what qualifies, at what level of evidence)
  - Explicit exclusion examples with brief rationale for each
  - A seed list of 6+ known platforms to anchor the research and calibrate the inclusion boundary
  - A target range of 15–30 platforms for the review corpus
